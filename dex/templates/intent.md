# Feature Intent: <title>

> Written before any research. Describe the problem, not the solution.
> If a section does not apply, write "Not applicable" rather than inventing content.

## Problem

What problem are we solving? Who is currently blocked, and by what?

## Desired Outcome

What should be true when the work is finished? Describe observable behavior,
not classes or tables.

## User / Actor

Who experiences the behavior? A human role, a calling service, a scheduled job.

## Scope

What is included in this change?

## Non-Goals

What is explicitly outside this change? Be specific — this is the section that
prevents scope creep during implementation.

## Constraints

Known technical, business, regulatory, compatibility, or operational constraints.

## Acceptance Signals

Observable outcomes that indicate success. Prefer things that can be checked:
a response shape, a persisted record, an emitted event, a visible state.

## Unknowns

Questions that should not be silently decided. Anything answered here by
guessing is a decision someone will have to live with.
